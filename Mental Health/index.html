UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

The Problem

University life can bring challenges that affect students' emotional and psychological well-being.

Academic Pressure

Exams, assignments and high expectations can create constant stress and anxiety.

Lack of Balance

Balancing school, personal life, work and relationships can become overwhelming.

Fear of Asking for Help

Some students avoid seeking support because of stigma, fear of judgment or lack of information.

MAKE A CHANGE

What can we do?

Supporting mental health starts with small, everyday actions.

Take care of yourself

Make time for rest, hobbies, exercise and activities you enjoy.

Support others

Listen without judging and encourage friends to seek help when needed.

Break the stigma

Talk openly about mental health and remember that asking for help is a strength.

YOU DESERVE SUPPORT

It's okay to ask for help.

Reach out to someone you trust, a mental health professional or an available student support service.
